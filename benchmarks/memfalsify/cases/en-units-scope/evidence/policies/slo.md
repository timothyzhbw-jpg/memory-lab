# Service level objectives

Effective 2026-06-01.

The search service's p99 latency budget is 250 milliseconds.

The billing service's p99 latency budget is 800 milliseconds.

A checkout write must return within 2 seconds.

The monthly error budget for the search service is 0.1 percent of requests.

A page fires when the search error rate stays above 1 percent for 5 minutes.

The billing service's monthly error budget is 0.5 percent of requests.
