# Release 3.8 (2026-07-14)

## Removed

- GPX export was removed from the web app and the mobile apps. Fewer than 1% of accounts used it in the last 90 days.

## Changed

- Route sharing links now expire after 30 days.
- The route list loads in pages of 50 instead of 200.

## Notes

- KML export is unchanged and remains available on every plan.
