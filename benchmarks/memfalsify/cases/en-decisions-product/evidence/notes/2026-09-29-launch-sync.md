# Mobile 4.0 launch sync, 2026-09-29

Attendees: Dana Whitfield, Priya Raman, Hannah Ilves, Leila Haddad

## Scope

- The 4.0 release date stays 2026-10-20 for both app stores.
- Offline mode is out of scope for 4.0 and moves to 4.2.
- 4.1 is a bug-fix release with no new features.
- iOS 4.0 raises the minimum supported version to iOS 16.
- Android minimum version: no change planned for 4.0.

## Route optimization v2

- Route optimization v2 stays disabled by default for Enterprise accounts and is switched on per account when the customer asks.
- In the pilot, route optimization v2 cut dispatcher planning time by 12%.
- Pricing for route optimization v2 pilots has not been decided.

## Payments
