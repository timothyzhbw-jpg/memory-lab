# Plans and prices

Effective 2026-04-01. Prices are in USD and exclude tax.

- Starter: free for up to 3 drivers.
- Team: $39 per seat per month billed annually, or $45 per seat billed monthly.
- Business: $49 per seat per month billed annually, with SSO and the dispatcher console included.
- Enterprise: custom pricing, minimum 200 seats.

A seat is an active driver in the billing month.
