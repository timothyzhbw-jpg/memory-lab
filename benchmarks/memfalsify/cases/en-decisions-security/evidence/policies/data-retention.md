# Data retention policy

Version 3, approved by Sven Aaltonen (security lead) on 2026-06-12.

## Retention periods

- Raw GPS pings: deleted after 30 days.
- Aggregated route history: deleted after 13 months.
- Driver chat messages: deleted after 90 days (in force since 2026-01-15, previously kept for 1 year).
- Invoices: kept for 7 years.
- Support tickets: deleted 2 years after the ticket is closed.

## Access

- Support agents see aggregated route history only. Raw pings are restricted to the data platform team.

## Deletion requests

- Deletion requests are completed within 30 days. Requests covered by a legal hold are paused until the hold is lifted.
