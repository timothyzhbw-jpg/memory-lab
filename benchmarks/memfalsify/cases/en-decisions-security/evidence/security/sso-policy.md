# SSO and provisioning policy

Owner: Sven Aaltonen. Last updated on 2026-09-24.

- SAML SSO is required for Enterprise workspaces created on or after 2026-07-01.
- Enterprise workspaces created before that date may keep password login until their next renewal.
- Business workspaces can turn on Google sign-in. SAML SSO is not available on Business.
- SCIM provisioning is available on Enterprise only.
